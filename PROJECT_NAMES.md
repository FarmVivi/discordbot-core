# Science Fiction Inspired Project Names

Here are creative project name suggestions inspired by your favorite sci-fi universes:

- **TARDISBot** - Time And Relative Dimensions In Space Bot
- **SonicBot** - Like the sonic screwdriver, versatile and useful
- **GallifreyCore** - Named after the Time Lords' home planet
- **RegenerateBot** - Like the Doctor, constantly evolving
- **VortexBot** - References the time vortex
- **Cyberman** - Upgraded and enhanced (though maybe too dark?)
- **TimeLordBot** - Master of time and space
